## 1. What do you sell, and who is it for?

Gut is a webapp where people with gut problems track their meals, symptoms, bathroom habits and notes. An AI/LLM reads the patterns and gives them a report on what's likely causing their intolerances, allergies, or possible illnesses (as suggestions, not a medical diagnosis), so they can adjust their diet or decide to see a specialist. There's also "Gut", an AI chatbot you can talk to instead of just filling forms, and it can pull Apple Watch activity to show calories consumed vs. remaining. It's for adults (30+) with ongoing gut discomfort who want to understand it but can't or don't want to pay for an expensive GI specialist, and who are willing to log consistently.

## 2. How did it start, and where are you taking it?

I started it because I struggle a lot with food and how it sits in my gut, and I wanted a smart tool to actually understand what was wrong — so I built one using AI/LLMs plus a structured way to track everything. I noticed other apps either cost a lot or just store your logs without giving any real outcome. I want Gut to be the app people with gut issues reach for to finally understand what they feel and improve or even overcome it. I'd feel I made it if it reliably delivers accurate insights in days rather than the month or two it takes now.

## 3. What makes you different, and what do you believe about your industry?

Unlike apps that only log data and leave you to interpret it, Gut turns everything you log into an actual report and clear insights, and you can talk to an AI chatbot instead of only filling forms. It's affordable rather than premium-priced, and it's built by someone who actually lives with gut problems. I believe logging is useless if nothing reads the data back to you — the value is the insight, not the log. Understanding your gut shouldn't need an expensive specialist. And a health tool should stay honest about its limits: suggestions and a picture, never a fake diagnosis.

## 4. How does the business run today?

It's at launch stage — still a personal project, running locally, not deployed yet, with no pricing or channels defined. I'm a solo founder working on it part-time. I want it paid but affordable — not free, but not too expensive — though I haven't set pricing yet. Next steps: deploy it, define pricing, buyer personas and channels, and make the time-to-insight shorter.

## 5. One thing true about you a competitor couldn't say?

It's built by someone who personally lives with gut problems, not an outsider to the issue — and it turns your data into real insights and a conversation, while staying clear of medical claims.

## 6. Customer / tester feedback (survey question)

A close friend (30+, health-conscious, cares about his gut) found it very interesting and enticing, and suggested adding features for food amount/portion per meal. An acquaintance (30+) thought it was cool and liked the AI features and how it fits a healthy lifestyle; she said she wasn't very aware of what she was eating but would like to be, and thought the app could help with that.
