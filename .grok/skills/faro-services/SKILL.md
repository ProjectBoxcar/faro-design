# Faro services (short)

Answer in **one tight block** — no audit, no code tour, no setup lecture unless asked.

## What Faro Design is

Local-first web app that turns a few business answers into a **complete, strategy-grounded brand package**: strategy → name → logo → visual system + channels → handoff → content.

## Services (six stages — all required)

1. **Strategy** — Interview + Express draft (concept, brief, manifesto, design plan); you edit and approve. (Full pillar map stays optional depth.)
2. **Brand name** — Confirm or pick the spelling logos will lock.
3. **Logo Workshop** — AI logo candidates; you choose and approve.
4. **Design Studio** — Identity system, landing page, brand deck, **and** SMS / email / ad / print templates — all from strategy + approved identity.
5. **Brand Handover** — Downloadable package + private client share when all seven finals are selected.
6. **Content Studio** — Month of social posts from real media, after the full package; journey complete when a calendar with posts exists.

## What you leave with

- A strategy you can say out loud  
- Locked name + approved logo  
- Identity + landing + deck + channel templates  
- A content month grounded in that brand  
- Files for builders / a client link
