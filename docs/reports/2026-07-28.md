## 1. Product context

**What it is**  
Faro Design is a guided brand-building web app for business owners. It takes plain answers about the business and produces a full brand package: AI-drafted strategy, logo concepts, design system, landing page, deck, mockups, and a shareable handover for a designer — grounded in the Finisterra methodology.

**How the journey works**  
Owner path: **start / express intake → strategy pillars → logo workshop → design studio → brand handover** (share link + downloadable assets). Progress is shown as stages with locked / next / done.

**Current product posture**  
Active build on `main`, pilot-ready for end-to-end brand packages. Focus this period: complete delivery (implement pack + reliable design lane) and a clearer owner journey.
